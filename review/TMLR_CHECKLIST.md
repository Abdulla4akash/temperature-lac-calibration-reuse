## Prepared

The manuscript uses the current official anonymous TMLR LaTeX and bibliography styles, unchanged. It is 13 pages including references and complete proof appendices. Appendices follow references. TMLR has no fixed page limit, but length must be justified. The draft does not invent authors, affiliations, funding, acknowledgment, ethics approval, code license or external validation. Its PDF author field is empty. Necessary limitations and a factual generative-AI assistance disclosure remain.

An anonymous computational supplement is provided separately from author-facing review notes. It is well below the 100 MB supplementary limit. The paper's generic TMLR review header is template-generated; this package has not been submitted.

## Required author decisions before upload

Confirm the actual author list, substantial intellectual contributions and accountability under TMLR's authorship policy. Check names, affiliations, funding, conflicts and ORCID/profile information in the submission form. All authors must have the required active OpenReview profiles. Do not add AI tools as authors. Review the current eligibility, submission-limit and reviewer-service rules; this preparation did not inspect anyone's OpenReview account or prior submission history.

Confirm no prohibited concurrent submission or overlapping archival publication; preprint policies differ from archival overlap rules. Confirm the intended license and consent to submission. Do not assume a code license or funding declaration has been chosen by the preparer. Assess any relevant ethical risks rather than asserting a nonexistent ethics approval. Verify the AI-use disclosure against actual use and take human responsibility for every theorem, numerical statement and citation.

Review the final main PDF and supplement for identity clues, including any additions made after this preparation. Use the PDF and anonymous supplementary ZIP, not the full author review bundle. Submit and communicate only through the journal process when ready. No journal contact or submission was authorized or performed here.

## Technical decision

No identified substantive proof gap or required new experiment remains. Author review and truthful policy declarations are essential; a new NLL theorem, finite-precision result or benchmark study is optional future work, not a condition imposed by this audit.

The paper's literature-based originality claim remains subject to peer review. An equivalent earlier softmax characterization could affect originality, but no such subsuming theorem was identified among the inspected sources. Repeated AI review is not independent human validation.
